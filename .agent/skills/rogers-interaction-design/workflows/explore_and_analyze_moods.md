# Workflow: Explore and Analyze Moods

This workflow guides you through designing and conducting user mood exploration and analysis, leveraging digital journaling and mood-tracking technologies to promote user well-being.

## 1. Preparation: Define the Mood Tracking Strategy
- **Determine the tracking mechanism:** Choose how users will record their feelings (e.g., manual journaling, simple rating scales, or rich media uploads).
- **Select the primary metrics:** Decide if you are tracking immediate affective states (momentary happiness) or reflective states.
- **Identify the platform:** Consider whether a mobile application (e.g., Moodnotes, Daylio) or an immersive environment (e.g., VR Mood Worlds) best suits the context.

## 2. Execution: Implement Data Capture
- **Prompt timely entries:** Design sporadic or context-triggered prompts to capture the user's mood accurately in the moment.
- **Enable rich descriptions:** Allow users to add subject lines, context descriptions, photos, and videos to provide depth to their emotional state.
- **Support intuitive rating:** Use clear, engaging visual scales (like stacked cards or smiley happiness ratings) to lower the barrier for input.

## 3. Reflection: Facilitate Emotional Generalization
- **Prompt periodic reflection:** Ask users to occasionally review and reflect on their past mood entries.
- **Highlight patterns:** Design the system to visually surface patterns, helping users understand why they might feel overly ecstatic or gloomy.
- **Promote positive framing:** Encourage users to relive positive experiences and overcome negative ones by actively writing them down and drawing lessons from them.

## 4. Refinement: Evaluate the Emotional Impact
- **Assess well-being outcomes:** Measure if the double act of recording and reflecting successfully increases user well-being and happiness.
- **Iterate based on user feedback:** Refine the prompts, rating scales, and reflection intervals to ensure the tracking process remains engaging and not burdensome.
